Character sprite sheets from the Flare collection on OpenGameArt.org, used as published

Each sheet here is the right-facing row of the original 8-direction sheet, re-packed into an 8-column grid
with client/scripts/pack-sheet.mjs. The game flips the art for the left-facing direction. The authoritative
list is client/src/content/assets.ts; the game shows these credits in Settings -> Credits. The rest of the
cast is rendered by this project: see ../olympus/CREDITS.txt.
