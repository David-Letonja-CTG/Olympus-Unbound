Character sprite sheets rendered by the OLYMPUS UNBOUND project

These sheets were rendered in Blender by this project (art/blender/ in the repository: the scripts, sources and
steps) from the following works, licensed CC-BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) or CC0. They are
adaptations: recoloured, combined with other parts and extended with new modelled parts. The game shows these
credits in Settings -> Credits; the authoritative list is client/src/content/assets.ts.
