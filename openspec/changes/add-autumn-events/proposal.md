# Proposal

## Why

The owner asked (2026-10-26) for a seasonal event in every month of the year. The winter and spring–summer changes brought December to July. This one fills the last gaps, August, September and November:

| Window | Holiday | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08-06 – 08-19 | the Perseid meteor shower | The Stars of Perseus: Medusa |
| 09-15 – 09-28 | the grape harvest and autumn feasts | The Vintage of Dionysus |
| 11-16 – 11-30 | the harvest feasts of late November (Thanksgiving) | The Hunger of Erysichthon |

With the Night of Hecate in October, every month now has an event.

## What Changes

Each event follows the earlier pattern: a realm outside the chain with its landmark, four monsters and a boss (recoloured sheets, D15), an event weapon and relic, a currency with its own picture, a shop of three rewards and two achievements.

- **The Stars of Perseus** (August 6 – 19): the *Isle of the Gorgons*, a rocky shore crowded with heroes turned to stone, under falling stars. Medusa's immortal sisters guard her and must be cut in order. With both down she is exposed. Her gaze sweeps in slow, heavy cones. Items: *Shield of Perseus* and the *Winged Sandals*. Currency: meteor stones.
- **The Vintage of Dionysus** (September 15 – 28): the *Vineyards of Naxos*, rows of vines and the pirates' ship overgrown with ivy. Two panthers guard Dionysus and never return once slain. Below half health, the vines close in. Items: *Thyrsus* and the *Cup of Dionysus*. Currency: grapes.
- **The Hunger of Erysichthon** (November 16 – 30): the *Grove of Demeter*, autumn wheat round the goddess's felled oak. Erysichthon swings the axe and rushes. Below half health, his hunger draws the gods to his mouth. Items: *Sickle of Demeter* and the *Cornucopia*. Currency: wheat sheaves.
- **A year of events:** every month has at least one event day, and no two events overlap (a requirement and a test).

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `seasonal-events`: the three events, and a year of events.

## Non-goals

- Thanksgiving by its real date (the fourth Thursday of November): the window stays fixed over the second half of November.
