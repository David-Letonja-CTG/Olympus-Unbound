# Proposal

## Why

The second of the four realms the owner chose (2026-10-11): the Lernaean Marsh, home of the Hydra. Every enemy and the boss come from the Greek myth of Heracles at Lerna.

## What Changes

- **The Lernaean Marsh**:
  - **Look:** the poisoned swamp of Lerna; bog, mud and reedbeds around deep water, lit by green will-o'-wisps in a cold green mist.
  - **Places:** sunken shrines of mossy marble, a drowned temple, broken walls, cypress groves, reeds, lily pads.
  - **Order:** it follows the Underworld in the chain of realms and can be chosen as the starting realm.
- **Its creatures**:
  - **Swamp serpents** (the Hydra's brood): fast, weaving and numerous.
  - **Giant crabs** (the brood of Karkinos, the crab Hera sent against Heracles): slow, tough and heavy-hitting.
  - **Lamiae**, the serpent-tailed: they coil, then lunge.
  - **Naiads** of Lerna's poisoned springs: they keep their distance and spit venom.
- **The boss: the Hydra**, after 7 minutes in the realm:
  - **Attacks:** its many heads bite all around it, venom rains on circles under the players, and its brood is called.
  - **Below half health** ("cut one head and two grow back"): faster, a larger brood and more venom, and it lunges.
  - **Gold:** 320.
- **Art**:
  - Lamia and Naiad are rendered from Flare's heroine; the Lamia gets a modelled serpent tail.
  - The swamp serpent, giant crab and Hydra are rendered from methodical pixel's CC0 animated snake and crab on a Flare stage (D15 as amended). The Hydra's five necks are snakes standing on a modelled body.
  - New code-drawn marsh scenery and the venom projectile.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `realm-lerna`: the Lernaean Marsh, its creatures and the Hydra.

## Non-goals

- The Aegean Shore and the Labyrinth of Crete (their own changes).
- Water you can't walk through, or poison that lingers on the ground.
