# Proposal

## Why

The owner asked (2026-10-26) for more seasonal events, at least one in every month of the year, chosen from the most common holidays. The Night of Hecate (Halloween) proved the event system: a realm, a host, a boss, items, a currency and a shop.

The year is planned as eleven new events in three changes. This one brings the four from December to March. Each holiday is told through a Greek myth, as the game's world is Greek:

| Window | Holiday | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12-15 – 01-05 | winter solstice, Christmas, New Year | The Longest Night: Nyx |
| 01-10 – 01-31 | deep winter | The Winds of Boreas: the North Wind |
| 02-07 – 02-21 | Valentine's Day | The Festival of Eros |
| 03-10 – 03-24 | St Patrick's Day (green and gold), spring equinox | The Golden Apples: Ladon in the Garden of the Hesperides |

Later changes: April (Pan's revels, April Fools and Easter), May (Artemis' hunt, May Day), June (Apollo and Python, midsummer), July (the theft of fire, fireworks), August (Perseus, the Perseids), September (the vintage of Dionysus, harvest) and November (the hunger of Erysichthon, Thanksgiving).

## What Changes

- **Four events**, each with:
  - a realm outside the chain, with its own landmark, trees, props and lights;
  - a host of four monsters and a boss with its own mechanic (recoloured sheets, D15);
  - an event weapon and relic;
  - a currency with its own picture, and a shop of three rewards;
  - two achievements.
- **The Longest Night** (December 15 – January 5): the *Starlit Wood*, a snowbound pine wood hung with lamps. Nyx and her twin sons, Hypnos (Sleep slows the gods near him) and Thanatos, guard her. *Stars of Nyx* and the *Veil of Nyx*; star shards.
- **The Winds of Boreas** (January 10 – 31): the *Peaks of Thrace*, where frozen lakes slide underfoot and hot springs heal. Boreas charges on the gale and is dazed when he hits a wall. *Breath of Boreas* and the *Cloak of the North Wind*; hailstones.
- **The Festival of Eros** (February 7 – 21): the *Garden of Aphrodite*, rose terraces by the sea. Eros' golden and leaden arrows guard him; with both broken he is exposed. *Arrows of Eros* and the *Girdle of Aphrodite*; roses.
- **The Golden Apples** (March 10 – 24): the *Garden of the Hesperides*, under Atlas holding up the sky. Ladon stays coiled round his tree, his heads striking on their own rhythm. *Apple of Discord* and the *Burden of Atlas*; golden apples.
- **Every event's currency has its own picture**, on the ground and in the menus (honey cakes stay Hecate's).
- **Snow** joins the realms' weather.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `seasonal-events`: the four events; each currency's picture.

## Non-goals

- Moveable feasts by their real dates (Easter, Lunar New Year, Thanksgiving): windows stay fixed month-days.
- New renders: the cast is recoloured from the existing sheets (D15).
- A separate high-score table per event: runs of every event share the event table.
