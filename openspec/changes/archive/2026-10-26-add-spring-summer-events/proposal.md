# Proposal

## Why

The owner asked (2026-10-26) for a seasonal event in every month of the year. The winter change brought December to March. This one brings April to July:

| Window | Holiday | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 04-01 – 04-14 | April Fools' Day, the Easter season | The Revels of Pan |
| 05-01 – 05-14 | May Day, the spring in bloom | The Hunt of Artemis: the Calydonian Boar |
| 06-15 – 06-28 | the summer solstice | Midsummer of Apollo: Python at Delphi |
| 07-01 – 07-14 | the fireworks of early July | The Theft of Fire: the Caucasian Eagle |

The last change brings August, September and November.

## What Changes

Each event follows the winter pattern:
- a realm outside the chain, with its own landmark;
- four monsters and a boss with its own mechanic (recoloured sheets, D15);
- an event weapon and relic;
- a currency with its own picture, and a shop of three rewards;
- two achievements.

- **The Revels of Pan** (April 1 – 14): the *Woods of Arcadia*, spring oaks and reed-fringed ponds that slow. Pan's shout spreads panic in cones. His four echoes strike from round the arena and cannot be hurt. Below half health, the reeds of Syrinx rise. Items: *Pipes of Pan* and the *Reeds of Syrinx*. Currency: painted eggs.
- **The Hunt of Artemis** (May 1 – 14): the *Forest of Calydon* in flower under the moon. The Calydonian Boar charges and gores, and a charge into a wall dazes it for 3 seconds. Items: *Moonbow of Artemis* and the *Hide of the Boar*. Currency: silver arrowheads.
- **Midsummer of Apollo** (June 15 – 28): the *Slopes of Delphi*, sun-baked terraces with leaning columns. Python's coils draw the gods toward its maw. Items: *Rays of Apollo* and the *Lyre of Apollo*. Currency: laurel leaves.
- **The Theft of Fire** (July 1 – 14): the *Peaks of the Caucasus*, where Prometheus hangs in chains, vents erupt and the sky bursts with coloured flashes. Three stolen fires guard the Caucasian Eagle and burn the gods near them; the eagle dives. Items: *Stolen Fire* and the *Fennel Stalk*. Currency: embers.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `seasonal-events`: the four spring and summer events.

## Non-goals

- Easter by its real, moving date: the window stays fixed.
- New engine mechanics: every boss uses existing ones.
