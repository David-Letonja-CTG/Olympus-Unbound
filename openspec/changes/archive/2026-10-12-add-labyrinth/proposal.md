# Proposal

## Why

The last of the four realms the owner chose (2026-10-11): the Labyrinth of Crete, the maze Daedalus built beneath Knossos, where Asterion, the Minotaur, waits at its heart. Every enemy and the boss come from Cretan myth. It closes the chain: after the Labyrinth the party returns to Mount Olympus, harder.

## What Changes

- **The Labyrinth of Crete**:
  - **Look:** an underground maze of dressed stone, warm low torchlight in drifting dust and embers; no moon.
  - **Places:** walls of stone blocks that form a maze over the whole map, with breaches and knocked-down walls so there is always more than one way round. Rooms of red Minoan columns and a shrine with the bull altar open in it. Horns of consecration, torches, bones of the tribute, straw and labrys floor tiles.
  - **Ariadne's thread:** a red thread runs along the road from where the party enters to Asterion's arena.
  - **Order:** it follows the Aegean Shore in the chain of realms (and leads back to Mount Olympus); it can be chosen as the starting realm.
- **Its creatures**:
  - **Kouretes** (Curetes), the armed dancers who hid the infant Zeus, now the maze's cursed guard: quick and in packs.
  - **Bronze Guardians**, the kin of Talos: slow, very tough, hard-hitting.
  - **Snake priestesses** of the Minoan serpent goddess: they keep their distance and hurl a curse of gold coils.
  - **Minotaurs** (already in the game), who charge.
- **The boss: Asterion**, after 7 minutes in the realm:
  - **Attacks:** his labrys sweeps all around him, the ceiling's stones fall on circles under the players, and he rushes along a telegraphed line.
  - **Below 60% health:** the Labyrinth answers its king: he calls minotaurs; he grows faster, with more falling stones and a wider sweep.
  - **Below 25%:** he goes berserk: faster still, rushing twice as often.
  - **Gold:** 380.
- **Maps:** a new point-of-interest rule, the maze: square cells carved into a maze with every cell reachable, some inner walls knocked down, some blocks missing; walls close enough that nobody fits between their blocks. Roads may carry a thread drawn along the road to the boss arena.
- **Art**:
  - The Curete, the Bronze Guardian and the snake priestess are rendered from Flare's hero and heroine.
  - Asterion is the Flare troll with a modelled bull's head, horns, a gold nose ring and his club made into a bronze labrys.
  - New code-drawn Labyrinth scenery (wall block, Minoan columns, horns of consecration, bull altar, torch, straw, labrys mosaic) and the serpent-curse projectile.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `realm-labyrinth`: the Labyrinth of Crete, its creatures and Asterion.

### Modified Capabilities

- `arena-generation`: the maze rule and the boss road's thread.

## Non-goals

- Enemies that find their way through the maze (they still walk straight at the gods and slide along walls).
- Walls that move, doors, keys or puzzles.
