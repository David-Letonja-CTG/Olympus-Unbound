# Proposal

## Why

The owner tried the richer synthesized sounds (2026-10-12) and did not like them: the sounds should be like their skills, "Zeus's storm should be the actual storm sound". Synthesis cannot sound like real thunder, water or stone, so the cues are made of recordings.

## What Changes

- **Recorded cues**, from CC0 recordings the owner approved:
  - Kenney's Impact Sounds and RPG Audio (impacts, stone, metal, punches, slices, coins);
  - lentikula's Basic Spell Impacts (lightning, fire, ice, water);
  - WuxiaScrub's Rain + Long Thunder (the thunder cut out).
- **What they become:**
  - **Zeus:** Wrath of Olympus is a lightning crack and a rolling thunderstorm; the thunderbolt a lightning bolt and a clap; the spear a crackle.
  - **Poseidon:** Tidal Wave is surging and crashing water; the trident splashes and rings.
  - **Athena:** Gorgon's Gaze is an ice shimmer, then cracking stone; her spear a ringing swish.
  - **Hades:** Gates of Tartarus is a slowed fire roar with breaking stone; the bident a dark chop.
  - **Other weapons:** the hammer is heavy blows on stone; the flaming sword a swish and a fire burst; the bow and scythe swish.
  - **Combat:** hits, crits, kills, elite and boss deaths, and your own hurt are real impacts; gold and chests are coins and a latch; the boss's falling stones land with stone and its rumble is distant thunder; the gate tolls a bell.
- Sounds keep their placement, variation, burst limits and limiter. Interface sounds (level-up, choices, synergies, fanfares) and enemy cues without recordings stay synthesized, and every cue falls back to its synthesized voices until its files have loaded.
- The files are listed in the asset manifest with their licences (D12); D9 is amended.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `hud`: sound feedback (abilities and weapons made of recordings of what they depict).
