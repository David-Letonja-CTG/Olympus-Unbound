# Proposal

## Why

The owner (2026-10-22): the menus are silent, which is "lame" when you come to play the game. Music starts only once a run begins.

## What Changes

- **The Hall theme:** a calm theme of the game's own outside runs. It is composed and synthesized by the game (D9) with the battle music's instruments.
  - An 8-bar lyre melody over a choir pad in D minor.
  - It plays once quietly, then again with a frame drum and bass, and loops.
- **Where it plays:** the main menu, god and realm selection, the co-op lobby, the Daily Trial and challenge screens, the Hall of Glory, progression, settings and the run summary.
- **Where it stops:** it fades out when a run or the intro starts, and fades back in afterwards.
- **Volume:** it follows **Music** volume, and 0% silences it.
- **Autoplay:** browsers allow sound only after the player's first click, key or touch, so the theme begins then.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `music`: the Hall theme outside runs.

## Non-goals

- A different theme per screen, or music during the intro (it has its own sound).
- Recorded music (D9).
