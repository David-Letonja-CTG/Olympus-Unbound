# Proposal

## Why

The third of the four realms the owner chose (2026-10-11): the Aegean Shore, where Scylla guards the strait. Every enemy and the boss come from Greek sea myth and the Odyssey.

## What Changes

- **The Aegean Shore**:
  - **Look:** moonlit wet sand, dunes and sea-rock above the sea, under a cool silver light with drifting spray.
  - **Places:** sea-worn marble temples and plazas, beached shipwrecks, spilled amphorae, beacon braziers, shells and seaweed.
  - **Order:** it follows the Lernaean Marsh in the chain of realms and can be chosen as the starting realm.
- **Its creatures**:
  - **Drowned sailors** (Odysseus's lost crew): slow and relentless, also in packs.
  - **Sirens:** keep their distance and hurl their song, as rose rings.
  - **Tritons**, Poseidon's mermen: they charge with tridents.
  - **Giant crabs** from the shore.
- **The boss: Scylla**, after 7 minutes in the realm:
  - **Attacks:** her six hounds bite all around her, Charybdis opens whirlpools under the players, and her tentacles lash along a line.
  - **Below half health:** the drowned rise to her call; she grows faster, with wider bites and more whirlpools.
  - **Gold:** 340.
- **Art**:
  - Siren, Triton (modelled fish tail) and drowned sailor are rendered from Flare's hero and heroine.
  - Scylla is the heroine, framed larger, with a ring of six hound heads cut from the CC0 wolf and six writhing tentacles from the CC0 snake (D15 as amended).
  - New code-drawn shore scenery (shipwreck, amphorae, shells, seaweed, sand) and the siren-song projectile.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `realm-aegean`: the Aegean Shore, its creatures and Scylla.

## Non-goals

- The Labyrinth of Crete (its own change).
- Sea you can wade into, tides or ships.
